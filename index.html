Welcome!

Jin has more than 10 years of professional experience, including 5 years of MES project management, 4 years of machine learning model application projects, 4 years of quality improvement consulting, 3 years in a large state-owned chemical enterprise, and 1 year in a foreign-invested company. Result-oriented and a strong team player, Jin focuses on smart manufacturing, data-driven quality improvement, and the practical application of AI in the tobacco industry.

Jin currently works at China Haisum Engineering Co.,Ltd. as a Product Manager in the Smart Manufacturing Department. Jin is dedicated to the exploration and practical implementation of integrating process optimization with AI in the tobacco industry.

Previously, Jin worked as a Project Manager in the Smart Manufacturing Department. Jin led the MES upgrade and renovation project for Shanghai Cigarette Factory — a contract valued at over RMB 10 million with a project team of more than 30 members — participating in the full lifecycle from pre-sales to final acceptance. Jin also served as Project Manager for the Beijing APS retrofit project and the Tianjin Cigarette Factory PES retrofit project. In these roles, Jin was responsible for overall project management, including client communication and coordination, project planning and execution, monitoring and reporting, and the management of quality, risks, issues, changes, and delivery, as well as technical direction control, team building, team performance management, and long-term client relationship development.

Prior to that, Jin worked as Consulting Manager and Leader Consultant, specializing in quality improvement for the tobacco industry. Jin led the Shainin DOE quality improvement project at Wuhan Cigarette Factory (2 sub-projects), the introduction and application of Shainin methods at Changsha Cigarette Factory (5 sub-projects), and the intelligent quality management and improvement collaboration project at Nanning Cigarette Factory (Phase I). For Shanghai Cigarette Factory, Jin delivered the full-production-process quality analysis projects (Phase I and Phase II), the Shainin methodology introduction and application project, and sci-tech innovation projects, and was responsible for schedule control, data analysis deliverables, stakeholder management, and the building of the company's project case knowledge base.

Jin also has rich hands-on experience as an expert consultant on machine learning and big data projects. This includes building typical SPCD machine learning models at Wuhan Cigarette Factory, applying PFMEA to improve cigarette production quality at Enshi Cigarette Factory, auxiliary identification of tobacco impurities on the primary processing line, and exploring an intelligent prediction and control model for stable primary processing strength based on big data mining technology at Shanghai Cigarette Factory. Earlier in Jin's career, Jin worked as a Process Technology Sales Engineer at a foreign-invested trading company in the Asia-Pacific region, and as a Storage and Transportation Process Engineer at Sinopec Shanghai Gaoqiao Company, where Jin was in charge of process design review, production organization, safety operating procedures, and the technical training of a 20-person team.

Jin holds the CAQ Six Sigma Black Belt certification and the PMI Project Management Professional (PMP) certification, and is proficient in MS Office, Visio, Project, Minitab, and JMP, with deep expertise in Six Sigma, DOE (including Shainin DOE), PFMEA, SPC, and machine learning modeling and application. Jin holds a Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering and Technology from Beijing University of Chemical Technology. Jin is now focusing on industrial AI agents and data governance in smart manufacturing, helping factories turn process data into stable quality and continuous improvement.
